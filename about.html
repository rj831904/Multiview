📖 About MultiViewer

Watch More. Miss Nothing.

MultiViewer is a free, browser-based tool that lets you watch multiple YouTube videos side by side — no downloads, no accounts, no limitations. Just paste your links and start watching.

What is MultiViewer?

Everything you need to know about the simplest way to watch multiple YouTube streams at once.

🎬

Multi-Stream Made Simple

MultiViewer is a lightweight, browser-based application that allows you to watch two or more YouTube videos simultaneously in a clean, responsive grid layout. No software to install, no browser extensions required — it works right in your browser tab.

🆓

Free Forever

MultiViewer is and always will be completely free to use. There are no premium tiers, no feature gates, and no hidden costs. We built this tool because we needed it ourselves, and we believe everyone should have access to it without opening their wallet.

🔒

Private & Secure

We don't collect personal data, we don't require you to create an account, and we don't track your viewing habits. Your video selections stay in your browser. MultiViewer respects your privacy by design — not as an afterthought.
