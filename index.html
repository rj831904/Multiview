📖 How to Use the Multi Viewer Tool

ℹ️ Note: This tool embeds standard YouTube iframes. It is 100% free, requires no login, and works on mobile, desktop, and tablets.

Step-by-Step Guide:

  • Step 1: Copy the URL of any YouTube Video or Shorts.
  • Step 2: Paste it into the input box above.
  • Step 3: Choose the number of player windows (1–50).
  • Step 4: Select your audio preference — muted runs smoother on mobile.
  • Step 5: Click "Generate Players" and watch!

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcut

Press Enter while the URL field is focused to quickly generate players.
