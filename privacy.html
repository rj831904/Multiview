🔍 Overview

MultiViewer ("we," "our," or "the service") is a free, browser-based tool that allows users to watch multiple YouTube videos simultaneously. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website.

🛡️ MultiViewer is built with a privacy-first approach. We do not require account creation, we do not store personal data on our servers, and all video processing happens entirely within your web browser.

By using MultiViewer, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please discontinue use of the service.

📋 Data Collection

We are committed to collecting as little data as possible. Here is a full breakdown of what we do and do not collect:

What We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal identifiers — no names, email addresses, phone numbers, or physical addresses
  • Account credentials — we do not have an account or login system
  • Payment information — MultiViewer is completely free to use
  • Video watch history — your viewing activity is never logged on our servers
  • IP addresses — we do not log or store visitor IP addresses

What May Be Collected Automatically

  • Basic usage analytics — anonymized page view data and general traffic metrics (see Analytics section below)
  • Browser-stored preferences — layout settings and theme preferences may be saved in your browser's local storage to improve your experience. This data never leaves your device.

Local Storage & Browser Data

MultiViewer may use your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences, such as your preferred grid layout or volume settings. This data is stored entirely on your device, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared at any time through your browser settings.

🍪 Cookies & Storage

MultiViewer itself does not set any first-party tracking cookies. However, third-party services integrated into the site may set their own cookies:

  • YouTube Embeds — When you load YouTube videos, YouTube (Google) may set cookies to provide video playback functionality, track preferences, and gather analytics. These cookies are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google AdSense — In the future, we may integrate Google AdSense to support the continued development of MultiViewer. If enabled, AdSense may place cookies on your browser to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can manage ad personalization settings at Google Ads Settings.
  • Google Fonts — We use Google Fonts to deliver consistent typography. Google may collect basic request data (such as your IP address) when fonts are loaded. See Google Fonts Privacy FAQ for details.

💡 You can control cookie behavior through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, although this may affect functionality of embedded YouTube players.

🔗 Third-Party Services

MultiViewer integrates with the following third-party services to deliver its core functionality:

YouTube (Google LLC)

MultiViewer uses YouTube's embedded player (iframe embeds) to display video content. By using MultiViewer, you are also subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy. YouTube may collect data about your interactions with embedded videos, including playback events and viewing duration.

Google Fonts

We load the "Plus Jakarta Sans" typeface from Google Fonts to ensure a consistent visual experience across devices. When your browser requests these font files, Google receives standard HTTP request data. Google states that it does not use this data for tracking or building advertising profiles. For more information, visit the Google Fonts Privacy FAQ.

Future Integrations

We may integrate additional services in the future (such as Google AdSense for advertising). Any new third-party integrations will be reflected in an updated version of this Privacy Policy, and the "Last Updated" date will be revised accordingly.

📊 Analytics

MultiViewer may use Google Analytics to understand general usage patterns and improve the service. If enabled, Google Analytics collects anonymized data such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • General geographic region (country/city level, not precise location)
  • Device type, browser, and operating system
  • Referral source (how you arrived at the site)

Google Analytics uses cookies to distinguish unique visitors. This data is aggregated and anonymized — it cannot be used to identify individual users. We use this information solely to understand how MultiViewer is used and to guide improvements.

You can opt out of Google Analytics entirely by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

🔒 Data Security

Since MultiViewer processes everything client-side and does not store personal data on servers, the attack surface for data breaches is inherently minimal. Nevertheless, we take the following precautions:

  • The website is served over HTTPS to encrypt data in transit
  • No server-side databases store user information
  • All user preferences are stored locally in the browser and are under the user's full control
  • We regularly review our codebase and third-party dependencies for security issues

⚖️ Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, we believe you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Right to Access — You can view all locally stored data through your browser's developer tools (Application → Local Storage).
  • Right to Deletion — You can clear all MultiViewer data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage and cookies for this site.
  • Right to Opt Out — You can opt out of analytics tracking by using browser extensions like the Google Analytics Opt-out Add-on, or by enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.
  • Right to Information — You have the right to know what data is collected. This policy serves as our full disclosure.
  • Right to Portability — Since all data is stored locally in your browser, you already have full control and access to it.

For EU/EEA Residents (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union or European Economic Area, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Since MultiViewer does not collect or process personal data on its servers, GDPR obligations are minimal — but we remain committed to respecting the spirit of the regulation.

For California Residents (CCPA)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal information is collected, request its deletion, and opt out of its sale. MultiViewer does not sell personal information to any third parties.

👶 Children's Privacy

MultiViewer is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since our service does not require account creation or any form of personal data submission, the risk of inadvertent collection is minimal. If you believe a child has somehow provided personal information through our service, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.
